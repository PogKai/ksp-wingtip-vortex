WingtipVortex 1.4.1 — Smooth wake
Download: [WingtipVortex 1.4.1](https://github.com/PogKai/ksp-wingtip-vortex/releases/tag/v1.4.1)
SpaceDock: [WingTipVortex](https://spacedock.info/mod/4209/Wingtip)
A patch release:

* Fixed the vortex tube drawing a sawtooth, one tooth per frame, in a hard pull at speed. It showed from the side in any 7+ g pull and got worse with speed.
* Fixed spiral breakdown re-winding every frame at jet speed.
* Wing vapor is now spread more evenly over the wing and rests on its upper surface instead of hanging half below it.

Drop the `WingtipVortex` folder into `GameData`, replacing the old version.
KSP 1.12.x · Stock aero or FAR (tested with FAR 0.16.2)
