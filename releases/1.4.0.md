# WingtipVortex 1.4.0 — FAR

**Ferram Aerospace Research is now supported.** Until now FAR removed the stock lifting modules the
mod finds wings by, so under FAR there were no vortices and no wing vapor at all. 1.4.0 reads FAR's
own per-wing forces, so both effects follow the angle of attack, lift and stall FAR actually flies
the wing at. High-altitude contrails also spread out toward their end the way real ones do, and a
sideways zigzag on fast, hard-pulling jets is fixed.

## Highlights

### FAR support

* Wings are found by FAR's modules as well as stock's, and each wing's force is read from it by
  reflection. **FAR is not a dependency**: without it nothing changes
* Vortex strength comes from the wings' lift only, not FAR's whole-vessel force with fuselage lift
* Wing vapor reads each wing's own lift and stall: a stalled wing loses its leading-edge suction
  peak, so vapor thins past the stall
* Under FAR the vapor uses physics FAR makes meaningful: the suction peak is capped where the flow
  over the wing would go supersonic, and clipped wing panels are read at FAR's own lift coefficient
* Tested with FAR 0.16.2
* **Under stock the wing vapor behaves exactly as in 1.3.0**; the FAR-specific parts only run when
  FAR is detected

### Contrails spread near their end

A real high-altitude contrail is a tight, sharp line while the wake's vortex pair holds the ice in
its cores, then widens fast and thins once the pair breaks up. In the contrail band the last half
of the trail now does the same, to about 3x its width, fading as it spreads.

## Fixed

* **Vortices zigzagged, seen from the side, on a fast jet in a hard pull.** A many-part wing flexes
  on KSP's springy joints, up to 2 m at the tip, and the wake recorded every wobble. It now follows
  the wingtip on the rigid airframe
* **The tube's twist aliased at jet speed** into a sawtooth; the cross-section now rounds off where
  its rings are spaced too far apart to twist cleanly
* **The tube's inward curve followed the live load**, so a wobbling load shifted it ring to ring

## Install

Download `WingtipVortex_1.4.0.zip` and drop the `WingtipVortex` folder from it into `GameData/`. To
upgrade, replace the old `WingtipVortex` folder. No dependencies, no configuration, no GUI.

## Compatibility

* KSP 1.12.x. Developed and tested on 1.12.5, including a heavily modded install with BDArmory 1.13.1
* **Stock aerodynamics or Ferram Aerospace Research** (tested with FAR 0.16.2)
* Body-relative: nothing is keyed to altitude in metres, so it is intended to behave the same on any
  atmosphere (only Kerbin has been flown)

## Known limitations

* Vortices are drawn for at most ten craft at once, the nearest to you
* Wing vapor is drawn on the craft you fly only
* Under stock, tailerons and elevons ignore the roll share of their load for wing vapor, and under
  both a surface lifting against the aircraft makes none. Both are style choices, not physics
* KSP has no weather, so the air's moisture for wing vapor is a fixed stand-in. Slow, high-alpha
  vapor, which real jets only show on very humid days, does not appear

## Reporting a problem

Search `KSP.log` for `[VORTEX]`; lines carry the craft they are about, as `[VORTEX] [<craft>] ...`,
and the log says at start whether FAR was detected. For the detailed wing-vapor log, create an
empty file named `verbose.txt` in the `WingtipVortex` folder (next to `Plugins`), fly, and include
the `[VORTEX] wing vapor:` lines.
