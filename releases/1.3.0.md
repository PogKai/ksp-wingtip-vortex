# WingtipVortex 1.3.0 — Wingmen

1.2.0 added wing vapor and real wake physics, all of it on the one aircraft you were flying.
**1.3.0 draws every aircraft in the air around you**, so an AI wingman or a BDArmory opponent trails
its own vortices, and the vortices themselves now form the way real ones do: a thin, faint thread at
the wingtip that thickens and brightens behind it.

## Highlights

### Every aircraft gets its vortices

* Each craft has its own vortex controller: the one you fly, plus other loaded aircraft with lifting
  surfaces, nearest first, up to ten in all
* **Switching vessels no longer touches any wake.** The craft you leave keeps its trails, and the one
  you take over already has its own
* Debris, EVA kerbals, flags and BDArmory missiles are skipped
* Wing vapor stays on the craft you fly and moves with you when you switch. It is the expensive
  effect, a per-part aerodynamic solve every physics step, so it is not handed out to every craft

### BDArmory

* BDArmory makes a fired missile the active vessel to follow it. The wing vapor no longer follows it
  there: it stays on the aircraft that fired it while you watch the shot
* AI craft spawned in the air are measured correctly (see Fixed below), so their vortices match what
  the same craft would draw starting from the runway
* Recognised by module name only: there is still no dependency on BDArmory

### How the vortices look

* **Thin and faint at the wingtip.** Width and opacity both build up over the first part of the
  rope, and the brightest stretch sits behind the aircraft instead of at the tip. Real tip-vortex
  condensation starts wispy and thickens as the core rolls up, and a thin tube of vapour is fainter
  than a wide one
* **Spiral breakdown toned down.** It used to lay a regular corkscrew hundreds of metres long. It is
  now a gentle kink that throws a turn or two and disperses, which is what a real spiral breakdown
  does

### Wing vapor

* **Rolling no longer fogs the tail.** A taileron, elevon or all-moving stabilator makes vapor only
  from the load it shares with its mirror twin: a pure roll deflects the two halves equal and
  opposite, so neither fogs, while a pull still fogs both. Stock hands a small, fully deflected
  surface far more lift than a real tail carries, so one tail half used to fog at every roll input

## Fixed

* **Vortices and wing vapor dimmed every afternoon**, not just at night: with the sun low they were
  drawn at a half to a third of their midday strength under a still-bright sky. The light now
  follows whether the sun is up, fading through twilight
* **Span measured wrong on a craft that wasn't level**, as much as 1.8 times too wide. It came from
  world-aligned bounding boxes, which only fit a craft lined up with the world axes, as one roughly
  is on the runway. Switched to in flight, or spawned in the air, a craft got the wrong circulation,
  core size and breakdown. It is now measured in the craft's own frame
* **Wingtip anchors could land on the wrong part**, from the same cause
* **Every wake drawn twice at flight start**, and kept that way all flight by a craft that started
  in the air
* **Wing vapor could switch itself off in combat** after a part was shot off at the wrong moment

## Install

Download `WingtipVortex_1.3.0.zip` and drop the `WingtipVortex` folder from it into `GameData/`. To
upgrade, replace the old `WingtipVortex` folder. No dependencies, no configuration, no GUI.

## Compatibility

* KSP 1.12.x, stock aerodynamics (`ModuleLiftingSurface` / `ModuleControlSurface`). Developed and
  tested on 1.12.5, including a heavily modded install with BDArmory 1.13.1
* Body-relative: nothing is keyed to altitude in metres, so it is intended to behave the same on any
  atmosphere (only Kerbin has been flown)
* **Not compatible with Ferram Aerospace Research.** Vortex placement and the wing vapor detect the
  stock lifting modules FAR removes

## Known limitations

* Vortices are drawn for at most ten craft at once, the nearest to you. Each one rebuilds its own
  tube meshes every frame, so a very large BDArmory match costs more than a duel
* Wing vapor is drawn on the craft you fly only
* Tailerons and elevons ignore the roll share of their load for wing vapor, and a surface lifting
  against the aircraft makes none. Both are style choices, not physics
* KSP has no weather, so the air's moisture for wing vapor is a stand-in: a fixed dew-point spread

## Reporting a problem

Search `KSP.log` for `[VORTEX]`. Lines now carry the craft they are about, as
`[VORTEX] [<craft>] ...`. For the detailed wing-vapor log, create an empty file named `verbose.txt` in
the `WingtipVortex` folder (next to `Plugins`), fly, and include the `[VORTEX] wing vapor:` lines.
